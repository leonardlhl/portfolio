Personal statement

I view architecture as the art of problem-solving, blending design with rational logic and order. This philosophy shapes my approach, focusing on efficient workflows through strategic pre-planning and standardization. Above all, I excel at pinpointing the key aspects of each project, ensuring every design decision effectively meets its core needs.
